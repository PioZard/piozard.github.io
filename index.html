PioZard

Brain games for kids and grown-ups –
and a lab for new ideas.

PioZard is a one-person studio from Poland, made with a love for learning. We build small games that train memory and logic, learning apps created with teachers in mind, and a lab where we try new ways to play.

📚 Learn – for kids and grown-ups

Learning apps for children and brain games for everyone. Apps made for kids follow these rules:

  • No accounts and no chat. Progress stays on the device.
  • Purchases and links outside the app are behind a parental gate.
  • If an app shows ads, they are family-safe and never personalized.

Zegarek – learn to tell time

Turn the minute hand and watch the hour hand follow. Six levels, from full hours to the 24-hour clock, with Polish time phrases like „wpół do ósmej”.

🧪 Lab – playful experiments

Small games for everyone where we try new ideas and new ways to play. Family-friendly, no violence, no adult content.

Skate Finger

A fingerboard in your pocket: your fingers become the skater. Ollies, flips and grabs with real finger gestures.
