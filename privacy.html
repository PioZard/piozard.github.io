Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile applications published by PioZard on Google Play handle data. We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, photos or location. Details for each app are listed below.

1. Applications covered

  • Zegarek – learn to tell time (com.piozard.zegarek) – for children; no ads

2. What data we collect

Our apps have no accounts, no registration and no chat. We operate no servers, and the apps do not send any data to us. Apps marked “no ads” above contain no third-party SDKs that collect data.

The only things saved are game progress (for example stars earned) and whether the full version has been unlocked. They are stored locally on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

3. Speech

Apps may read time phrases aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on the device (or recorded audio included in the app). Text is processed on the device; the app does not record or use the microphone.

4. Advertising

Some apps may show ads served by Google AdMob. Whether an app shows ads is stated in the list in section 1. In apps for children, ad requests are marked as child-directed: ads are never personalized, there is no remarketing, and only family-safe ad content is allowed, as required by the Google Play Families Policy. AdMob may process limited device data to serve and measure ads; see how Google uses data.

5. Purchases

Some apps offer a one-time purchase that unlocks additional levels. Purchases are placed behind a parental gate and processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive payment details. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children’s privacy

Our apps for children comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under COPPA (USA) or the GDPR (EU). Purchases and links outside the app are placed behind a parental gate.

7. Permissions

The apps request no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, location or contacts). Apps with ads use internet access only to load ads.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
