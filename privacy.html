Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile applications published by PioZard on Google Play handle data. Our apps are made for children, so we keep it simple: we do not collect, store or share any personal information.

1. Applications covered

  • Zegarek – learn to tell time (com.piozard.zegarek)

2. What data we collect

None. Our apps have no accounts, no registration, no chat and no analytics. We operate no servers, and the apps do not send any data to us.

The only things saved are game progress (for example stars earned) and whether the full version has been unlocked. They are stored locally on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

3. Speech

Apps may read time phrases aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on the device (or recorded audio included in the app). Text is processed on the device; the app does not record or use the microphone.

4. Advertising

Our apps for children show no advertising and contain no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

5. Purchases

Some apps offer a one-time purchase that unlocks additional levels. Purchases are placed behind a parental gate and processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive payment details. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children’s privacy

Our apps are designed for children and comply with the Google Play Families Policy. Because we collect no personal information, no data about children is collected under COPPA (USA) or the GDPR (EU). Links to this policy or to the store are placed only behind the parental gate.

7. Permissions

The apps request no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, location or contacts). Vibration may be used for gentle feedback during play.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
