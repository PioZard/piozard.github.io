Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

In short
  • We never ask for names, emails, photos or location.
  • No accounts, no chat. Game progress stays on the device.
  • Some apps show ads from Google AdMob. In apps for children they are never personalized.
  • Purchases go through Google Play, behind a parental gate.

1. Applications covered

This policy applies to the following apps published by PioZard on Google Play:

  • Zegarek – learn to tell time (com.piozard.zegarek)
    for children · may show ads (child-directed, non-personalized)

2. What data we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store personal information such as names, email addresses, photos, contacts or location. Our apps have no accounts, no registration and no chat. We operate no servers, and the apps do not send any data to us.

Game progress (for example stars earned) and whether the full version has been unlocked are stored locally on the device and are deleted when the app is uninstalled.

3. Advertising

Some apps show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may process limited device data (such as the advertising ID, IP address and device type) to serve ads, limit how often they appear and prevent fraud. See how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

In apps for children, every ad request is marked as child-directed (COPPA) and under the age of consent (GDPR). This means:

  • ads are never personalized and there is no remarketing,
  • only ads rated suitable for families (rating G) are allowed,
  • only ad networks certified for the Google Play Families program are used.

4. Purchases

Some apps offer a one-time purchase that unlocks additional content. Purchases are placed behind a parental gate and processed entirely by Google Play. We never receive payment details. Google’s privacy policy applies to the payment: policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Speech

Apps may read text aloud using the text-to-speech engine installed on the device or recorded audio included in the app. The apps do not use the microphone.

6. Children’s privacy

Our apps for children comply with the Google Play Families Policy. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under COPPA (USA) or the GDPR (EU). Purchases and links outside the app are placed behind a parental gate.

7. Permissions

The apps request no sensitive permissions (no camera, microphone, location or contacts). Apps with ads use internet access only to load ads.

8. Your rights

Because we hold no personal data, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You can reset ad data on your device in Android settings (Google → Ads), and remove all app data by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with an updated date.
